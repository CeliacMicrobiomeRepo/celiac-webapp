# Comprehensive cross-cohort analysis reveals global gut microbiome signatures of celiac disease
#### Peter J. Prendergast, Haig V. Bishop, Craig W. Herbold, Elena F. Verdu, Renwick C. J. Dobson, Andrew S. Day and Olivia J. Ogilvie

---

**About the study**

This published cross-cohort analysis used 963 stool and duodenal samples from the CMR to investigate gut microbiome signatures across prospective, active, and treated celiac disease. The study included diversity analyses, differential abundance testing, and machine learning prediction of celiac disease.

---
